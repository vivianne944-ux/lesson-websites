Reversible vs Irreversible Cell Injury

Same Concept, New FramingCell Injury
5appearances2022, 2024, 2025, 2026, 2028

Master Rule

Reversible injury retains recoverable membrane and organelle integrity. Irreversible injury is marked by severe membrane damage, mitochondrial or lysosomal failure, and nuclear breakdown.

Examiner Pattern

The examiner repeatedly contrasts early swelling and membrane blebbing with membrane rupture or nuclear degradation.

Same Concept, New FramingCell Injury
5appearances2022, 2024, 2025, 2026, 2027

Master Rule

Dry gangrene is predominantly ischemic coagulative necrosis without superimposed infection. Wet gangrene adds infection and liquefactive destruction to ischemic tissue.

Examiner Pattern

Look for cold pulseless black digits for dry gangrene versus infected black tissue with purulent exudate for wet gangrene.
