1. Autacoids: Core Concept and Classification Must knowPage 1
Must know
  • Autacoids are endogenous molecules with complex physiologic and pathophysiologic functions. They can have potent pharmacologic effects when administered as drugs.
  • The lecture groups them into amines, vasoactive peptides, eicosanoids, and nitric oxide.
  • Ergot alkaloids are not autacoids. They are fungal products that act at serotonin, α-adrenergic, and dopamine receptors.

Vasoactive peptidesPeptide mediators acting on vessels and other tissuesAngiotensin, bradykinin, substance P, BNP, CGRPThink constrictor vs dilator balance

Common confusion: The PDF labels amines and vasoactive peptides as “proteins” in its classification list. Preserve the lecturer’s classification for this lesson. Do not use ergot alkaloids as an example of an endogenous autacoid.

Recall Check

What are the four lecture groups of autacoids?
Amines, vasoactive peptides, eicosanoids, and nitric oxide.

No. They are fungal products and are discussed because they act on several autacoid-related receptors.

Must know

Where histamine is stored

  • Mast cells and basophils, especially at sites of potential tissue injury
  • ECL cells of the stomach, where it activates acid-producing parietal cells
  • Brain, where it participates in neuroendocrine control, body weight regulation, and sleep or arousal
Should know

Effects of administered histamine

  • Triple response after intradermal injection: red spot + edema + flare
  • Larger doses: flushing, urticaria, hypotension, tachycardia, headache, bronchoconstriction, and GI upset
  • Scombroid fish poisoning is a lecture example of histamine excess
  • Histamine itself has no clinical application in this lesson. Current drugs are receptor blockers.

Physiologic antagonist

Epinephrine for anaphylaxis. It acts at adrenergic receptors to oppose histamine actions.

Release inhibitors

Cromolyn, nedocromil, β2 agonists are listed for asthma.

Receptor antagonists

H1 for allergic conditions, H2 for acid peptic disease, H3 for narcolepsy, H4 unavailable clinically.

6. Ergot Alkaloids: Receptors, Uses, and Ergotism Must knowPages 4–5
Must know
  • Ergot alkaloids are complex molecules produced by Claviceps purpurea, a fungus that infects wet grains.
  • They may act as agonists, partial agonists, or antagonists at 5-HT1A, 5-HT1B, 5-HT1D, 5-HT2, α-adrenoceptors, and dopamine D2 receptors.
  • They act prominently in the blood vessels, uterus, and CNS.

Should know
  • Oral absorption is variable. Oral dose may be about 10 times the IM dose. Caffeine improves oral absorption.
  • Most common toxicity is GI disturbance: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea.
  • Other effects include drowsiness, hallucinations, and uterine contractions, especially in late pregnancy.
  • Chronic methysergide can cause connective tissue proliferation, hydronephrosis, cardiac murmur, and valvular damage. It was withdrawn from the US market.
Exam trap “St. Anthony’s fire” describes ergotism with fiery pain and severe vasospasm. Ergotamine is not safer than triptans.

Recall Check

Which drug is first line for postpartum hemorrhage in the lecture?
Oxytocin. Ergonovine is an alternative when oxytocin fails.
What is the most dangerous ergot overdose effect?
Prolonged vasospasm, which can cause gangrene, amputation, or bowel infarction.
What vasodilators are listed for ergot poisoning?
Nitroprusside or nitroglycerin infusion.

12. Nitric Oxide: Synthesis, Signaling, and Pharmacologic Manipulation Must knowPages 9–10
Must know
  • NO is a gaseous signaling molecule that readily diffuses across cell membranes.
  • It is synthesized from L-arginine by nitric oxide synthase in an O2- and NADPH-dependent reaction.
  • It binds iron in heme and can be inhibited by hemoglobin.

Last-Minute 5-Minute Cram Sheet

Receptor code

Histamine: H1 = Gq, H2 = Gs, H3/H4 = Gi. Serotonin: 5-HT1 = mostly Gi, 5-HT2 = Gq, 5-HT3 = ion channel, 5-HT4 = Gs.

Histamine

First-generation H1 blockers sedate and block autonomic receptors. Second-generation blockers are less sedating. H1 blockers treat chronic urticaria and allergic rhinitis, not asthma. Cimetidine inhibits CYP450. Pitolisant is an H3 inverse agonist for narcolepsy.

Serotonin

Triptans activate 5-HT1B/1D, reduce CGRP release, and constrict intracranial arteries. Lasmiditan activates 5-HT1F without vasoconstriction. Prucalopride is a 5-HT4 prokinetic. Ondansetron is a 5-HT3 antiemetic. Alosetron treats IBS-D but may cause major constipation. Serotonin syndrome = hypertension + hyperreflexia + hyperthermia.

Ergot

Not an autacoid. Ergotamine is less preferred than triptans. Ergonovine is only used when oxytocin fails in postpartum hemorrhage. Prolonged vasospasm can cause gangrene or bowel infarction. Treat severe vasospasm with nitroprusside or nitroglycerin.

Vasoactive peptide essentials

Renin → ANG I → ACE → ANG II → AT1 vasoconstriction. ACE also destroys bradykinin. ACE inhibitor cough and angioedema come from bradykinin. Avoid ACE inhibitors and ARBs in pregnancy. Bosentan treats PAH but may cause fatal hepatotoxicity. Icatibant blocks bradykinin B2 in HAE. Aprepitant blocks NK1 substance P for vomiting. BNP rises in heart failure. Sacubitril–valsartan is an ARNI for HFrEF.

Eicosanoids

Membrane → PLA2 → arachidonic acid → LOX or COX. Glucocorticoids inhibit PLA2. Zileuton inhibits 5-LOX. “-lukasts” block leukotriene receptors. Dinoprostone ripens the cervix. Misoprostol protects gastric mucosa and can induce abortion. Alprostadil treats ED and maintains PDA. Indomethacin or ibuprofen closes PDA. PGI2 dilates and opposes platelets. TXA2 constricts and aggregates platelets.

Nitric oxide

L-arginine → NOS → NO → guanylyl cyclase → cGMP → ↓Ca²⁺ → smooth muscle relaxation. Nitroprusside dilates arteries and veins. Nitroglycerin reduces preload and dilates coronaries. Sildenafil preserves cGMP. Never pair nitrates with sildenafil because lethal hypotension can occur.

Last trap sweep

H1 blockers do not treat asthma. Cimetidine is an inhibitor, not inducer. Ergonovine is not first line. Ergotamine is not safer than triptans. LTC4/LTD4 are bronchoconstrictors. Indomethacin closes PDA. NO is a vasodilator.
